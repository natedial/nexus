THROUGH-LINE SELECTION RUBRIC

## What is a Through-Line?
A through-line is a meta-narrative that emerges across multiple research documents. It synthesizes related themes and trades into a single actionable insight with clear source attribution.

It is not a topic bucket, a list of adjacent observations, or a trade dump. The collected themes should form the evidence spine of one coherent narrative.
Consensus is the framing layer. Contrarian views are worth elevating only when they challenge a shared market assumption that matters for pricing.

---

## Selection Criteria

### Prioritize (in order):

1. **Dominant consensus views that matter for market pricing**
   - Multiple sources align on direction, risk, or trade expression
   - Stronger signal than any single source alone
   - Example: "GS, JPM, and Citi all favor front-end receivers ahead of Fed pivot"

2. **Hidden fractures inside consensus**
   - Sources agree on destination but disagree on path, driver, timing, or trade expression
   - Example: "All expect curve steepening, but disagree whether it comes from front-end repricing or long-end rally"

3. **Conditional signposts that would flip consensus**
   - Identify the variable or threshold that would force a re-think
   - Example: "Consensus long-duration view breaks if refunding size or payrolls re-accelerate"

4. **Risk identification**
   - Surfaces threats to existing positions or consensus views
   - Highlights tail risks or underappreciated scenarios
   - Example: "Barclays and MS both flag Treasury supply as headwind for long-end"

5. **Contrarian views that challenge shared assumptions**
   - Non-consensus view from credible source
   - Must be specific and attack a prevailing market assumption, not merely disagree on detail
   - Example: "Deutsche argues AI will not be durably deflationary because policy offsets will dominate"

---

### Deprioritize:

- **Single-source without contrarian value**: One source saying something others don't mention (low signal)
- **Descriptive recaps**: Restating known events without forward implication
- **Vague or hedged**: "Markets are uncertain" or "It depends on data"
- **Off-topic**: Themes not relevant to rates, macro, or cross-market flows
- **Detached contrarian ideas**: Interesting challenges that do not connect back to the dominant market narrative

---

## Through-Line Structure

Each through-line must include:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `lead` | One-line summary with causal or conditional relationship (max 25 words) |
| `supporting_sources` | Array of source names |
| `consensus_level` | See consensus_rubric.md for definitions |
| `consensus_anchor` | One sentence naming the dominant market belief this through-line supports, fractures, or challenges |
| `supporting_themes` | Array of theme labels from input; usually 2+ themes for a real narrative |
| `supporting_trades` | Array of up to 2 short trade expressions (can be empty) |
| `key_insight` | Synthesis paragraph (max 120 words) covering market belief, narrative, agreement, disagreement, mechanism, flip signpost, implications |

---

## Quality Checks

Before finalizing a through-line, verify:

1. **Attribution**: Every claim cites supporting sources
2. **Specificity**: Lead includes concrete detail (not generic statement)
3. **Actionability**: A trader reading this knows what to do or watch
4. **Consensus level accuracy**: Follows rules in consensus_rubric.md
5. **Non-redundancy**: Doesn't overlap significantly with another through-line
6. **Narrative integrity**: Reads as one comprehensive finding tied back to the supporting themes
7. **Consensus tie-back**: Contrarian views explicitly name the shared assumption they challenge

---

## Target Output

- Produce 3-8 through-lines per synthesis
- Prefer quality over quantity
- Cover both consensus AND divergence where present
- Include at least one risk-identifying through-line if material risks are present
- Ensure at least half the through-lines are consensus-anchored
- Include at most 1-2 contrarian through-lines
