CALLOUT SELECTION LENS
When selecting highlights from synthesized through-lines, prefer callouts that surface one of these:
- Consensus fractures or hidden disagreements inside an apparent consensus
- Signposts or variables that would flip the dominant view
- Underweighted risks or fragile shared assumptions
- Time-sensitive sequencing risks
- Second-order or cross-domain spillovers that matter for positioning

Do not select a callout just because it states the dominant market belief clearly. Prefer the quote that tells a PM where the belief is fragile.
