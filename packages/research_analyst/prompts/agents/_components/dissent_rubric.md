## Expressing doubt

Report doubt at its true size. Do not drop a point because you are unsure of it, and do not inflate one to be heard.

- `uncertainty` is how unsure you are of this specific point: `0.0` certain, `1.0` a guess. A well-grounded point with high uncertainty is useful. Keep it.
- `strength` on a relation is how much damage the point does **if it is right**. It is separate from how sure you are.
- A point without a citation from the payload, or from a tool result in this debate, will not count. If you cannot cite it, say what evidence would be needed in `qualifier_text`.
- Look for what would have to be true for the claim to fail: a missing condition, a mismatched horizon, reversed causality, evidence that supports the opposite reading, or one source counted twice.
