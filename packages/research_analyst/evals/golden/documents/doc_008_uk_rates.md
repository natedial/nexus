## Full Text Excerpt

**UK Rates: MPC Divided, Markets Uncertain**

The Bank of England MPC remains deeply divided, with markets pricing only 30bp of cuts for 2026 - significantly less than the Fed or ECB.

**Key Points:**

1. **MPC Split**: Vote was 5-4 to hold, but several dovish members are close to switching

2. **Wage Dynamics**: Single month pay growth at 6.5% remains far above the 2% target. Services inflation at 5.3% is the key barrier.

3. **Growth Divergence**: UK underperforming peers. Q4 GDP at 0.1% vs 0.3% for Euro area.

4. **Political Noise**: Spring Statement provided little clarity on fiscal consolidation.

**Our View:**

We expect the Bank to remain on hold through Q2, with first cut in August. The path is highly data-dependent.

**Trade Recommendation:**

We like paying 2-year gilt futures. The market is pricing too few cuts. Our target: 4.00% on 2-year gilt (current 4.35%).

Alternatively, 5s30s steepener offers good risk/reward. Long 5-year, short 30-year.
