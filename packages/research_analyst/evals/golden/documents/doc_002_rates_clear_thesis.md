## Full Text Excerpt

The Federal Reserve is set to maintain its restrictive policy stance through the first half of 2026, with rates remaining at current levels until at least September. Our base case calls for the first cut in Q3 2026, driven by a gradual cooling in labor market conditions.

**Key Drivers:**

1. **Labor Market Moderation**: Job gains have slowed from 250k monthly average to around 180k, while wage growth has eased to 3.8% YoY from 4.1% peak.

2. **Inflation Progress**: Core PCE has declined to 2.6% from 2.8% peak but remains above target. We expect it to reach 2.4% by year-end.

3. **Financial Conditions**: Despite higher rates, credit conditions remain accommodative. Consumer spending has been resilient but showing signs of fatigue.

**Risk Scenarios:**

- *Upside*: Inflation falls faster than expected, enabling cuts in Q2.
- *Downside*: Labor market deteriorates sharply, triggering emergency cuts.

**Market Implications:**

We recommend positioned for a flatter curve through mid-year. The 2s10s spread should compress to 30bp as front-end remains anchored by Fed patience while long-end faces term premium pressure from supply dynamics.

The 10Y yield target is 4.15-4.25% for Q2, with tactical opportunities to add duration on dips below 4.00%.
