## Full Text Excerpt

**Commodities Outlook: Supply Constraints Meet Demand Resilience**

We maintain a constructive view on commodities, with oil and copper as top picks.

**Oil Market:**

The oil market faces a structural deficit. OPEC+ production cuts of 2.2M bpd have withdrawn more than 200M barrels from inventory over the past six months. Without additional supply, we see Brent reaching $95/bbl by Q3.

Key supporting factors:
- US Strategic Petroleum Reserve at 40-year lows
- Iranian exports face increasing sanctions pressure
- Chinese strategic buying intensifies

**Copper Thesis:**

Copper is our favorite industrial metal. The supply backdrop is the tightest in decades:

- Concentrate shortages limiting smelter output
- Grades declining at major mines
- Limited new supply coming online through 2027

Demand story is equally compelling:
- Data center build-out driving 500k tonnes incremental demand
- EV transition adds 400k tonnes by 2027
- Grid investment surge globally

Target: $5.50/lb by Q4 (current: $4.25)

**Gold:**

Gold remains a tactical trade. At current prices, the bull case requires:
- Further Fed cuts beyond market pricing
- Central bank buying to continue at current pace
- Geopolitical risk premium

We see more balanced risk/reward. Prefer silver on leverage.
