## Full Text Excerpt

**Our View: The Great Moderation 2.0**

We believe markets are on the cusp of a new regime - one characterized by lower volatility, controlled inflation, and sustained growth. Call it the Great Moderation 2.0.

This is not our base case - it is our conviction call.

**The Thesis:**

Central banks have mastered the art of soft landings. After the inflation shock of 2022-2023, policymakers demonstrated unprecedented coordination and credibility. The result: inflation normalize without recession.

**Why This Time Is Different:**

1. **Better Policy Framework**: Central banks now target average inflation, providing flexibility to overshoot temporarily.

2. **Structural Disinflation**: Technology, globalization 2.0, and deglobalization lite create persistent downward pressure on goods prices.

3. **Resilient Consumer**: Despite higher rates, consumers continue to spend. This reflects better balance sheet health coming into the cycle.

4. **No Alternative**: Bonds offer real yields but limited capital gains. Equities remain the only game in town for returns.

**Implications for Positioning:**

We are overweight risk assets across the board. Here's our playbook:

- **Equities**: 65% allocation, focus on quality and growth
- **Credit**: HY spreads to 275bp, IG to 110bp
- **Commodities**: Neutral, favor copper over gold
- **FX**: Dollar weaken to 102 DXY by year-end

**The Bear Case:**

We acknowledge the risk. If inflation proves sticky or the consumer cracks, we would see 15-20% correction. But the reward/risk favors being long.

This is the kind of market where being wrong but early hurts less than being right but late.
